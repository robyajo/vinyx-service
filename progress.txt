### Architectural Gaps Found
- `listener-stream` uses in-memory `@nestjs/event-emitter` instead of Redis PubSub — breaks scaling.
- No BullMQ integration for heavy processing (automation runtime, webhook retry).
- No healthcheck endpoints for WS, Redis, or services.
- No structured logging for WS, connector, automation events.
- No DTO validation (class-validator/class-transformer/ValidationPipe) in listener-stream NestJS app.
